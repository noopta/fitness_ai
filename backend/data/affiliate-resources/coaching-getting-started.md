# Axiom Coaching: Getting Started

Updated 19 October 2026

A trainer invites a client, the client accepts in the Axiom app, and the client appears on the trainer's roster. It takes about two minutes on each side.

## Before you start

|  | Trainer | Client |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | An Axiom account the Axiom team has enabled for coaching. Use the same sign-in you gave the team (for example Google). | Any Axiom account, free or Pro. |
| Where | The Axiom app or axiomtraining.io | The Axiom app (iPhone) |
| App version | Latest | Latest. Fully close and reopen the app once so today's update applies. |
| Cost | No subscription and a 0% platform fee | Free tier works; Pro adds the AI coach between sessions |

Coaching access for trainers is invite-only right now. If you coach clients and don't have it yet, email inquiries@axiomtraining.io.

## Trainers: sign in and set up your practice

**In the app**

1. If you're already signed in as a regular user, sign out first (You → Account).
2. On the sign-in screen, tap **Sign in as a personal trainer**.
3. Sign in with the account the Axiom team enabled (for example **Continue with Google**).
4. The first time, you'll see **Set up your practice**. Enter a name, for example "Kavi Strength", and tap **Create practice**. Clients see this name when you invite them.

**On the web**

1. Go to axiomtraining.io and log in with the same account.
2. Open axiomtraining.io/personal-training/clients.
3. Set up your practice the same way if prompted.

To use the app for your own training again, switch back from the dashboard; your trainer setup stays.

## Trainers: invite a client

1. In the dashboard, open **Clients** and choose **Invite a client**.
2. Enter the client's email if you know it. This is recommended: only that account can use the invite, and if they already have an Axiom account they get a notification and the invite appears in their chat automatically.
3. Tap **Create invite link**. You get two things, each with a copy button:
   - a **link**, and
   - a **code** like `K7QM-3XPA`.
4. Send both, by text or DM. The share button includes them in one message: "Join Kavi Strength on Axiom: \<link> (or enter code K7QM-3XPA in the app)".

Invites last **7 days**. If one expires, create a new one.

**Already have clients in a spreadsheet?** Use **Import** first. Your clients appear as "Not joined" with their history, and you send each one an invite from their profile when ready.

**In person at the gym:** show the client the code on your phone and have them type it into the app (see the next section). It's faster than sending a link.

## Clients: join your coach in the app

New to Axiom? Download Axiom from the App Store and create your account first. Then use whichever of these is easiest. They all end at the same screen.

**Option 1: the invite finds you (if your coach used your email)**

1. Open the Axiom app. You may also get a notification: "Kavi invited you to train with them". Tap it.
2. In the chat, Anakin says "Kavi invited you to train with Kavi Strength" and shows an invite card.
3. Read what Kavi will see, then tap **Accept**.

**Option 2: tell Anakin the code**

1. Open the Axiom app.
2. In the chat, type something like: "My coach gave me a code K7QM-3XPA". Capitals and the dash don't matter.
3. Anakin shows the invite card. Tap **Accept**.

**Option 3: enter it under You → Coach**

1. Open the **You** tab and tap **Coach**.
2. Enter the code or paste the link, then tap **Find invite**.
3. Read what your coach will see, then tap **Accept**.

## What your coach can see

Nothing is copied or sent to your coach. Once you accept, their dashboard reads your Axiom records directly, and only while you're coached by them. Stop sharing under You → Coach and their access ends immediately; nothing is deleted.

| Your coach sees | Your coach does not see |
| --- | --- |
| Workouts you log, with exercises, sets and notes, including ones from before you joined | Your chat with Anakin |
| Body weight (last 120 days) | Meal photos and individual foods |
| Daily nutrition totals: calories, protein, carbs, fat, and your calorie target | Messages with anyone else |
| Wellness check-ins: mood, energy, sleep and stress | Payment details |
| Injuries you've listed |  |
| Your program, and program changes Anakin has suggested that are waiting for you |  |
| Your name and email, your answers to their check-ins, and your messages with them |  |

## After joining

**Trainer: first 10 minutes with a new client**

- [ ] Check the client shows on **Clients** as **New**.
- [ ] Add or confirm their **injuries**. Axiom then refuses exercises that load an injured area until you clear it, in your programs and in their app.
- [ ] Set up **check-ins** (weekly or every two weeks, with your own questions).
- [ ] Give them a **program**: build one, or use **Generate with Axiom** for a first draft you edit.
- [ ] Optional: **bookings** (your weekly availability) and **billing** (connect your own Stripe; Axiom takes 0%).

From then on, the **daily briefing** tells you who needs attention and why. Suggested messages are drafts: nothing is sent until you press send.

**Client: what changes**

- Anakin, the in-app coach, now knows you have a trainer and points you to their check-ins when they arrive.
- Keep logging workouts, meals and body weight as normal; your coach sees them automatically.
- **You → Coach** shows who coaches you and exactly what they can see. **Stop sharing** ends their access at any time; your data stays yours and nothing is deleted.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Trainer sees "Personal training is not enabled for this account yet" | You're signed in with a different account from the one the Axiom team enabled (for example Apple instead of Google). Sign out and sign in with the enabled one, or email inquiries@axiomtraining.io. |
| Client has no **Coach** row under You, or Anakin doesn't recognise the code | The app hasn't picked up the latest update. Fully close Axiom (swipe it away), reopen it, then close and reopen once more. |
| Client is using the classic layout | Switch back to the standard layout (You → Account), or join through the invite link in a browser instead. |
| "This invite was sent to a different email address" | The trainer locked the invite to another email. The client signs in with that email, or the trainer creates a new invite for the client's actual email (or with no email). |
| "This invite is no longer valid" | It expired (after 7 days) or was already used. The trainer creates a new one. |
| Client accepted but isn't on the roster | Refresh Clients. Check they accepted with the account they use day to day; a second account would join separately. |
| Client opened the link and got a web page | That's expected for now. They can accept there by signing in with email or Google, or use the code in the app instead. |

Still stuck? Email inquiries@axiomtraining.io with the trainer's name and the invite code.
