# Axiom Affiliate Payment Structure

Axiom pays affiliates through four streams, all paid monthly in CAD.

## How you get paid

- Give people your code (for example SAEED10). They enter it when they subscribe: at Apple (App Store) checkout in the app, or at Stripe checkout on axiomtraining.io.
- Your code gives them the referral offer, and you earn 40% of every payment they make.
- The code is what credits the sale to you.

## The four streams

| # | Stream | What you earn | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Consumer Pro referrals | 40% of every Pro payment ($12.99/mo) | Lifetime of the subscription |
| 2 | Trainer referrals (paid trainer plans) | Commission on every paid trainer invoice at your agreed trainer rate | 12 months per trainer |
| 3 | Campaign / creative asset referrals | 30% of the plan price | Once, on the first paid invoice |
| 4 | Paid content commissions (UGC) | Per-video fee set in your agreement | Per accepted video |

Trainer plans: free up to 5 clients, then $49/mo (Coach) or $99/mo (Pro).

## Bonuses

Bonuses stack on top of your rates and never reduce them. The exact rungs are in your agreement.

- **Growth ladder**: One-time bonuses as your lifetime referred count crosses milestones.
- **Durability ladder**: One-time bonuses for referrals who reach a tenure milestone.
- **Fast-start**: A bonus for hitting an early referral target within your first 90 days.

## Payment rules

- All streams are paid monthly in CAD to your Stripe Express account.
- Subscriptions started with your code at Apple (App Store) or Stripe checkout are credited to you.
- Commissions earned before you finish Stripe onboarding are held for up to 12 months.
- You get 30 days' notice before any rate change.

## Your personal terms

Your personal rates appear in the "Your terms" card on your affiliate dashboard. Trainer rate, campaign rate, per-video fee and any agreement notes are shown there.
