# Axiom Coaching: Feature Guide for Trainers

Updated 19 October 2026

Axiom watches your roster every day, tells you who needs you and why, and drafts the message. You decide and press send. This guide gets you from your first client to a 10-minute daily routine.

## Your first week

Do these in order; each takes a few minutes.

- [ ] **Set up your practice** with the name clients will see. (See "Axiom Coaching: Getting Started".)
- [ ] **Bring in your existing clients.** Import your spreadsheet (.xlsx or .csv). Axiom shows what it found and imports nothing until you confirm. Clients appear as "Not joined" with their history.
- [ ] **Invite your first client** and have them accept in the app.
- [ ] **Confirm their injuries** on their profile, so unsafe exercises are blocked from day one.
- [ ] **Turn on check-ins**: weekly or every two weeks, with your own questions.
- [ ] **Give them a program**: build it, or let Axiom draft one you edit.
- [ ] **Open the daily briefing** the next morning and work through it once.
- [ ] Optional: set your **availability** for bookings and **connect Stripe** for billing.

## Your routine

| When | What | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every morning | Open the **briefing**. Handle each item: read why it's there, edit the suggested message, press send or dismiss. | ~10 min |
| When check-ins arrive | Open **Check-ins**. Read anything marked Needs attention or Worth a look; send all the Routine replies in one tap. | ~10 min a week |
| Weekly | Look at **Progress** for lifts marked Plateau or Regressing and adjust their programs. | ~15 min |
| Monthly | Review each client's **report**, add a personal note, then Approve and send. | ~2 min per client |
| Anytime | Ask **Anakin** a question about your roster instead of digging through profiles. | seconds |

## Every feature, and how to start with it

| Feature | What it does | How to start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clients (roster)** | Every client with a status (Might need support, New, On plan, Paused, Not joined) and an engagement trend (Rising, Steady, Falling). | Open Clients. Tap a client for their full profile. |
| **Daily briefing** | A short list of who needs you today and why: mentioned pain, waiting on a reply, a check-in worth a look, no session in a while, engagement dropped, high stress with low energy, a stalled lift, a new PR, missed check-ins. Each item has a suggested message. | Open Briefing each morning. Tap "Why am I seeing this?" to see the evidence. Nothing sends until you press send, and you get a few seconds to undo. |
| **Check-ins** | Scheduled questionnaires. Rules sort answers: any pain mention or two red answers is Needs attention; one red or two amber is Worth a look; the rest are Routine. | On a client, set a weekly or two-weekly schedule and your questions (free text, 1 to 5 scale, or a number). Clients answer from a link. |
| **Ask Anakin** | A read-only assistant for your roster. It answers from the same data as your dashboard and can draft messages, but it can never send anything. | Ask "Who mentioned pain in the last 14 days?" or "Who hasn't trained this week?". Questions can be set to run every morning. |
| **Programs** | A program builder with an exercise library, plus **Generate with Axiom** for a first draft of a training block you edit. | Open a client → Program. Start from Generate, then adjust. |
| **Injury safety** | Rules block any exercise that loads an injured area until you clear it. This applies to your programs, the briefing and the client's own app. | Confirm injuries on each client's profile; clear them when the client is ready. |
| **Progress** | Estimated 1-rep-max trend per lift, labelled Progressing, Plateau or Regressing, with how consistently they train. | Open Progress weekly and act on Plateau or Regressing. |
| **Monthly reports** | Sessions logged, adherence, PRs and body-weight change, calculated by rules, with a short summary. | Add your own note, then Approve and send. |
| **Nutrition** | Daily calorie and macro totals from the client's food logging, and the targets you set. | Open a client → Nutrition and set their targets. |
| **Notes and photos** | Private notes clients never see; progress photos by date and pose. | Add notes after sessions; ask clients to upload photos. |
| **Bookings** | Clients book your open slots themselves; each booking uses a session credit from their package; reminders go out the day before. | Set your weekly availability, then share it with clients. |
| **Billing** | Clients pay you directly through your own Stripe account. Axiom takes 0%; you pay only Stripe's processing fees. | Connect Stripe in Practice settings. If it shows as unavailable, ask the Axiom team. |
| **Import** | Bring clients and their history in from your spreadsheet; undo the whole import if needed. | Open Import and upload your .xlsx or .csv. |
| **Leads, retention and team** | A public profile with an enquiry form; a list of clients who might need you; extra trainers in your practice. | Find them in the dashboard menu once your roster is set up. |

## Tips that save time

- **Lock invites to the client's email.** If they already use Axiom, the invite appears in their chat on its own. You don't have to chase a link.
- **Onboard in person with the code.** Show the code on your phone at the gym; the client types it into the app and accepts on the spot.
- **Let the rules triage.** Only read Needs attention and Worth a look check-ins closely; clear the Routine pile with one tap.
- **Edit, don't write.** Suggested messages come from the facts in the briefing. A quick personal edit keeps your voice and takes seconds.
- **Schedule your Anakin questions.** "Who mentioned pain in the last 14 days?" set to run every morning becomes part of your briefing.
- **Your clients still get a coach between sessions.** Clients on Axiom Pro have Anakin in the app for day-to-day questions and logging, and everything they log shows up for you.
