# Getting Started with Axiom

Updated 19 October 2026

Axiom is your training and nutrition coach in one app. Anakin, the AI coach, builds your program, reads what you log, and suggests changes. Nothing changes until you say yes, and you can undo anything.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. **Download Axiom** from the App Store and create an account with Apple, Google or email.
2. **Answer the intake.** Anakin asks about your goals, health and injuries, training history, food, sleep and stress, schedule and equipment. Answer honestly: injuries and health answers decide which exercises you're given.
3. **Get your program.** Anakin builds a plan around your days, equipment and goals. Open **Training** to see this week.
4. **Log something today.** A meal (snap a photo) or your first session. Anakin learns from what you log.
5. **Optional: allow notifications** so Anakin can tell you when there's something worth your attention.

Have a trainer? Ask them for their invite code and see "Going further" below.

## Finding your way

Swipe left and right between five screens.

| Screen | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your chat with Anakin. Ask anything, log anything, and see suggestions as cards you can accept or decline. |
| **Training** | Today's session, your week, your program and its phases, past workouts, form checks and diagnoses. |
| **Fuel** | Today's food and macros, your targets, nutrient gaps and your gut-health score. |
| **Feed** | Friends, posts, messages and groups. |
| **You** | Strength profile, body weight, daily check-in, streak, what Anakin knows about you, your coach, billing, preferences and account. |

## Everyday use

**Log food in seconds**

- **Photo:** snap your plate; Axiom identifies the items and estimates calories and macros. Check and adjust before saving.
- **Barcode:** scan packaged food. If it's not found, photograph the nutrition label.
- **Describe:** type or say what you ate, for example "two eggs and toast" or "a Starbucks grande latte". Many chain restaurants' official menus are built in.
- Saved foods and recipes make repeat meals one tap.

**Train**

- **Follow your program:** open today's session in Training, log your sets as you go.
- **Log as you go (no program needed):** record whatever you did; Anakin still adapts from your trends.
- **Add past workouts:** pick a recent date, or paste your notes from another app and review them before saving.

**Talk to Anakin** (on Home)

Just type or speak naturally. A few things to try:

- "Log 3 sets of bench at 135 for 8."
- "I only have 30 minutes today, adjust my session."
- "My shoulder hurts on overhead press."
- "What should I eat to hit my protein tonight?"

When Anakin suggests a change to your program or targets, it shows a card with the numbers and the reason. **Nothing changes until you tap to accept**, and you can undo it afterwards.

## Your data and quick fixes

**You're in control**

- **What Anakin knows** (You tab) shows the notes Anakin keeps about you; remove any you don't want.
- **Units:** switch between kg and lb under You → Preferences.
- **Export your data** or **delete your account** from You → Account. Deletion is permanent and immediate.
- Your health information is used only to coach you. See the Privacy Policy and the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy at axiomtraining.io.

**Quick fixes**

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A new feature isn't showing | Updates download in the background. Fully close Axiom (swipe it away) and reopen it, twice. |
| A meal estimate looks wrong | Tap the meal to edit items or amounts, or tell Anakin "that was a small portion". |
| Anakin changed something you didn't want | Tap Undo on the card, or tell Anakin "undo that". |
| Something hurts | Stop the exercise, tell Anakin so your program adapts, and see a clinician if it persists. |
